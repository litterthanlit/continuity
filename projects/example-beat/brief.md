# Brief — example-beat

- **Subject:** Continuity now hears the music. A beat-synced product film made with the features it
  announces: scenes timed in bars, moves on the beat, whip-and-drift, squash and stretch, a morph
  that carries a card across a cut, and a wordmark that becomes the mark.
- **Audience:** design engineers and motion designers on X who have watched AI video drift off the
  beat.
- **The one thing to remember:** every cut and every hit lands on the music — and the agent can
  prove it (`ct beats`, the render's AV-sync check).
- **Format:** 16:9 · 1920×1080 · 30fps · ~12s · with sound (holds up muted).
- **Register:** energetic (docs/decisions/0007): whip, pop and steep eases, copy that wipes on,
  bursts answered by holds, one depth of morph instead of a cut.
- **Music:** 124 bpm, generated by `scripts/make-music.ts`: an intro bar, a groove with a riser
  into the morph, a boom where the stat lands, and two bars for the mark.
- **Copy:** ≤ 12 words on screen at once. Geist in three weights, two inks, litt's accent once.
