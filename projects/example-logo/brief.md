# Brief — example-logo

- **Subject:** Continuity's mark, as a material cut — the brand-film form Gal Shir made viral for
  Framer and Swan: one logo, about a second per shot, each shot a different material, every cut on
  the beat. This is the WebGL spike's proof (docs/decisions/0009).
- **Audience:** designers and design engineers scrolling X; the post is the video.
- **The one thing to remember:** the same mark, three materials, locked to the music — and it was
  made as code.
- **Format:** 16:9 · 1920×1080 · 30fps · 6s (3 bars, so it loops on the bar line) · plays with sound (and holds up muted).
- **Register:** energetic (0007): whip-and-drift moves, cuts on beats, bursts and a held end card.
- **Look:** litt-dark. Light comes from above (the studio environment's softbox). Materials are
  static presets — chrome, glass, neon — and the neon is litt's cobalt accent: colour that
  behaves like light. Geist for the wordmark.
- **Shots:** chrome (whips round to face us) → glass (bends litt's grid on a card behind it) → neon
  (glows, bloom) → the flat lockup, mark + wordmark, typed on the downbeat.
- **Music:** 120 bpm, generated by `scripts/make-music.ts` (deterministic synthesis), 2 beats per
  material shot, 6 beats for the end card.
